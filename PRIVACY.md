# Clipboard Manager Mobile Privacy Policy (draft)

Effective date: 8 September 2026

This draft describes the behavior of Clipboard Manager Mobile version 1.0.0.
Publish it at a stable public URL and review it against the final binary before
using it in Google Play or App Store Connect.

## Data handling

Clipboard Manager Mobile does not require an account and does not include
advertising, analytics, tracking, cloud synchronization, or an app-operated
backend. Snippets that you choose to save are stored locally on this device in
the app's private storage. They are not sent to the developer or a third-party
service by the app.

## Clipboard access

The app reads the device clipboard only after you tap **Read clipboard**. The
read text is placed in the draft editor; it is not transmitted. It is stored
locally only if you then tap **File snippet**.

The app writes a saved snippet to the device clipboard only after you tap
**Copy**. The app does not monitor the clipboard in the background.

## Sharing and external links

The app has no built-in sharing service. If you copy or share text into another
application, that application controls its own handling of the text. Links to
bookchaowalit.com and GitHub open an external website; the website's privacy
policy applies after you leave the app.

## Permissions and third parties

The app uses the operating system clipboard APIs and local storage. It does not
request location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or notification access.
The production configuration does not include a third-party analytics or
advertising SDK.

## Retention and deletion

Saved snippets remain in local app storage until you delete them with the
**Delete** action or remove the app according to the operating system's normal
uninstall behavior. The app has no developer-side copy to delete.

## Children

This is a general productivity tool and is not designed specifically for
children.

## Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the store privacy
declarations will be updated before the changed version is distributed.
