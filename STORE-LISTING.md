## Google Play

**App name**

Clipboard Manager

**Short description (80 characters or fewer)**

Save and find reusable text privately on your device.

**Full description**

Keep the useful line. Clipboard Manager is a private shelf for text you reuse.
File a snippet, find it again, and copy it back into your workflow.

Features:

- Save snippets locally on your device
- Read the clipboard when you choose
- Search saved text instantly
- Pin frequently reused snippets
- Copy a saved snippet with one tap
- Delete snippets whenever you want
- Use the app without an account, ads, or cloud sync

Clipboard Manager does not monitor the clipboard in the background. Clipboard
access happens only after you tap **Read clipboard** or **Copy**.

## Apple App Store

**Name**

Clipboard Manager

**Subtitle (30 characters or fewer)**

Private snippet shelf

**Keywords draft**

clipboard, snippets, notes, copy, paste, productivity, offline, local

**Promotional text draft**

Save the lines you reuse and retrieve them without an account or cloud sync.

**Description**

Clipboard Manager keeps reusable text close at hand. Type or read a snippet
when you need it, save it locally, search your archive, pin frequent entries,
and copy them back into another workflow.

The app does not monitor the clipboard in the background. It reads or writes
clipboard content only after you tap the related action. Saved snippets remain
on your device, with no account, advertising, analytics, or app-managed cloud
sync.

**App Review notes draft**

No account is required. On the Home tab, tap **Read clipboard** to import
current clipboard text, then tap **File snippet**. Use the search field, **Pin**,
**Copy**, and **Delete** actions to verify the primary workflow. The app does
not require a backend or special hardware.
